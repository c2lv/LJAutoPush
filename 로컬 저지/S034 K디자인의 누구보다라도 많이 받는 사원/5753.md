## 프롬프트

### 낸 코드

```
SELECT e.name AS employee, c.name AS company, a.pay
FROM employee e
JOIN affiliation a ON e.employee_id = a.employee_id
JOIN company c ON a.company_id = c.company_id
WHERE a.pay > (SELECT MIN(temp2.pay) FROM company AS temp1 JOIN affiliation AS temp2 ON temp1.company_id = temp2.company_id WHERE temp1.name = 'K디자인')
ORDER BY a.pay ASC
```
